FAQ

Parents ask. We answer.

Is this a physical book?

No. Kidventuro is a downloadable PDF designed to print at home, at work or at a local print shop.

Do I need an app?

No app and no child account. The finished adventure is a simple printable file.

Do you store my child's data?

The production version is designed to use only the minimum personalization data needed to create the book and to avoid building child profiles.

What ages is it for?

The first version targets ages 4–12, with activities adjusted by age group.
