FAQ

A few parent questions.

Is this a physical book?

No. Kidventuro is a digital printable adventure. Open it after payment, then use Print / Save as PDF.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No child account is needed. An adult completes the secure checkout.

What personal information do you need?

We use only a first name or nickname, age, destination, interest and trip length. Child names and exact ages are not sent to the AI provider.

Who is responsible for travel safety?

An adult must supervise the child and decide whether each mission is appropriate.

How quickly do I get the book?

After a successful payment, Kidventuro opens the personalized printable book so you can save it as a PDF or print it.
