FAQ

A few parent questions.

Is this a physical book?

No. Kidventuro is a digital PDF you can print at home or use from a tablet.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for this MVP.

What personal information do you need?

Only a child's first name, age, destination and interests are needed to personalize the book. We avoid collecting photos, birth dates or addresses.
