Panama City family guide

Panama City puts a world wonder within a taxi ride of your hotel: kids watch ships as long as streets rise and sink in the canal locks. The same day can hold sloths in a rainforest park, bike rides along the Amador Causeway and ice treats in the pastel plazas of Casco Viejo. Flights are short from the US, the currency is the dollar and the dry season runs January to April.

Stroller

The Cinta Costera seafront and the Amador Causeway are wide, flat and perfect for strollers. Casco Viejo has cobbles and narrow sidewalks, and many other streets have none, so pack a carrier too.

Top things to do in Panama City with kids

  1. Miraflores Locks Ages 4+

    From the viewing terraces, kids watch huge cargo ships lifted and lowered by water; check the transit times, and children under 6 enter free.

  2. Biomuseo Ages 6+

    Frank Gehry's bright-roofed museum shows how Panama rose from the sea and let animals cross between two continents, with big walk-through galleries.

  3. Casco Viejo Ages 3+

    Wander the restored old town to Plaza de Francia and the seaside walkway, buying a cold raspao or fruit ice pop on the way.

  4. Amador Causeway and Punta Culebra Ages 4+

    Rent family bikes on the causeway, then visit the Smithsonian's Punta Culebra Nature Center for sloths, turtles and touch pools.

  5. Gatun Lake boat tour Ages 4+

    Boats from Gamboa pass giant ships and islands where capuchin and howler monkeys live; choose operators that do not feed the wildlife.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Museo del Canal

    The canal's dramatic building story told inside an old building on Plaza de la Independencia.

  • Albrook Mall

    A giant air-conditioned mall with a carousel, indoor play areas and a big food court.

  • Panamá Viejo museum

    The visitor centre of the 1519 city ruins, then climb the old cathedral tower when it clears.

Getting around

Ride apps are the easiest way around and are inexpensive, while Metro Lines 1 and 2 are clean and air-conditioned with a rechargeable card. Gamboa and Gatun Lake are best reached on a tour that includes hotel pick-up.

Food kids like

Try patacones, carimañolas, fried hojaldra bread at breakfast, sancocho chicken soup and ceviche at the Mercado de Mariscos. Families lunch around 12-2 pm and dine about 7-8 pm, with air-conditioned breaks in between.

Safety notes

  • From May to November heavy afternoon storms with lightning are common, so plan outdoor visits for the morning and shelter quickly when thunder starts.
  • Traffic is heavy and drivers do not always stop at crossings, so hold hands near roads and use the footbridges where you find them.
  • Tap water in the city is treated and widely drunk, but use repellent against mosquitoes, especially near forest parks at dusk.
