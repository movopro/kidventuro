Why Paris works well as a kids’ activity adventure

Paris has famous sights around almost every corner, but young travelers do not always care that a landmark is famous. They care when it becomes part of a game. A clue to find on a bridge, a shape to notice on a façade or a food to spot in a bakery gives them a reason to look up and participate.

Kidventuro adapts activities to ages 4–12 and can reflect interests such as art, mysteries, food, history, science or trains. The booklet also includes quieter pages for the airport, train or restaurant table, so it helps during the less glamorous parts of the day too.
