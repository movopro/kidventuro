🇨🇱 Santiago with kids · Chile

Santiago family guide

Santiago is a modern capital with the snowy Andes as its backdrop. Kids ride a century-old funicular and a cable car over Cerro San Cristóbal, climb the castle-like terraces of Santa Lucía and eat warm empanadas. Within two hours you can splash in an Andean river, ride the old lifts of Valparaíso or, in the southern winter, play in real snow.

Best ages

Every age enjoys the hills, parks and science museums, and older kids get more from Valparaíso walks and river rafting, which operators usually allow from about 7-8 years.

Stroller

Providencia and Las Condes have wide, flat sidewalks and many Metro stations have lifts. The hills, Valparaíso's stairways and Santa Lucía's steps call for a carrier.

Top things to do in Santiago with kids

  1. Cerro San Cristóbal Ages 3+

    Ride the 1925 funicular from Pío Nono to the white Virgin statue, then take the cable car across the hill for wide views of the city and the Andes.

  2. Cerro Santa Lucía Ages 4+

    Climb stone stairways, terraces and a small fort on this free hill park in the centre; visitors register with ID at the gate.

  3. Cajón del Maipo Ages 5+

    About an hour southeast, families picnic by the Maipo River, spot condors over the peaks and try gentle rafting with local operators from spring to autumn.

  4. Valparaíso day trip Ages 6+

    Buses take about 1.5-2 hours to this colourful port, where kids ride the historic ascensor lifts and hunt for giant murals on the hills.

  5. Snow day at Farellones Ages 4+

    From about June to September the ski areas above Santiago offer snow play and beginner slopes about 1.5 hours away; book transport early on weekends.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Museo Interactivo Mirador

    A huge hands-on science museum in La Granja, easily a half day of experiments.

  • Museo Nacional de Historia Natural

    A free museum in Quinta Normal park with a whale skeleton and Chilean wildlife.

  • Sky Costanera

    The viewing deck of Gran Torre Santiago is clearest just after rain washes the air.

A simple first day

Morning
Ride the funicular up Cerro San Cristóbal for Andes views before the haze.
Afternoon
Eat at Mercado Central, then climb the terraces of Cerro Santa Lucía.
Evening
Have 'once' with sopaipillas and walk the cafés of Barrio Lastarria.

Getting around

The Metro is clean and quick with a rechargeable Bip! card, though very crowded at rush hour. Ride apps work well around the city, and a rental car makes Cajón del Maipo and the ski roads easier.

Food kids like

Kids love empanadas de pino, completo hot dogs with avocado, pastel de choclo and the summer peach drink mote con huesillo. Lunch runs 1-3 pm and dinner is late, so many families do 'once', an early evening tea with bread.

Safety notes

  • Summer sun from December to February is intense and dry, so carry water, hats and sunscreen, especially on the shadeless hilltops.
  • Pickpockets work in the centre, on the Metro and in Valparaíso, so keep phones and bags zipped and close in crowds.
  • Chile has frequent small earthquakes and builds for them, so learn your hotel's exit routes and teach kids drop, cover and hold on.
