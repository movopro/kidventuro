🇳🇴 Bergen with kids · Norway

Bergen family guide

Bergen sits between seven mountains and the sea, the gateway to Norway's fjords. Within minutes of the colourful wooden houses of Bryggen, a funicular lifts you to a forest with wooden trolls, lakes and playgrounds, and boats leave the harbour for waterfalls and narrow fjords. It rains often, so pack waterproofs, but on a clear day the views from Fløyen and Ulriken feel like the top of the world.

Best ages

Fløyen's troll forest and the aquarium delight children from about 3, while longer hikes, kayaks and the Ulriken ridge suit ages 8 and up.

Stroller

The harbour area is flat, but central streets climb steeply and Bryggen's alleys have uneven wooden boards, so a carrier is handy; Fløyen's main paths are gravel and stroller-friendly.

Top things to do in Bergen with kids

  1. Fløibanen to Mount Fløyen Ages 3+

    The funicular climbs to 320 metres in about six minutes, where the Trollskogen trail hides carved trolls, and there are playgrounds, a lake and canoes for hire in summer.

  2. Bergen Aquarium Ages 3+

    Watch penguins and sea lions at feeding time, see starfish and crabs up close and meet creatures from the Norwegian coast at the tip of the Nordnes peninsula.

  3. Bryggen and the fish market Ages 4+

    Wander the narrow alleys behind the old Hanseatic wooden houses, then look at king crabs and salmon at the harbour fish market, busiest in summer.

  4. Fjord cruise to Mostraumen Ages 4+

    A three-hour boat trip from the city centre squeezes through a narrow strait between steep cliffs and noses right up to waterfalls; bring warm layers for the deck.

  5. Ulriken643 cable car Ages 5+

    The cable car to Bergen's highest mountain gives views over the city, islands and sea, with easy short walks on the summit plateau for small legs.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • VilVite Science Centre

    Hands-on science centre with a loop bicycle, water play and weather experiments that fill half a day.

  • AdO arena

    Bergen's big indoor swimming arena with waterslides and a warm children's pool.

  • Bryggens Museum

    Medieval finds from the wharf, including wooden sticks carved with runic messages and old shoes.

A simple first day

Morning
Ride Fløibanen early and follow the Trollskogen trail at the top.
Afternoon
Walk down through the forest to Bryggen and explore the wooden alleys.
Evening
Eat fish cakes by the harbour and watch the boats come in.

Getting around

The centre is compact, and the Bybanen light rail runs from the airport to the city in about 45 minutes; Skyss buses and the light rail share tickets bought in the Skyss app.

Food kids like

Kids usually love fiskekaker (fish cakes), waffles with brown cheese and skillingsboller, Bergen's big cinnamon buns; try creamy Bergen fish soup at the Fish Market hall.

Safety notes

  • Bergen gets rain on around 200 days a year, so waterproof jackets, trousers and spare socks matter more than anything else you pack.
  • Mountain trails above Fløyen and Ulriken can be wet and foggy, so stay on marked paths and turn back early if the cloud comes down.
  • Harbour edges at Bryggen and the fish market have no railings, so hold small children's hands along the quay.
