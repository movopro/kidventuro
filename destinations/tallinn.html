Tallinn family guide

Tallinn's old town still has most of its medieval wall, with round towers and red pointed roofs that look straight out of a picture book. Beyond the walls the city is modern, green and easy: a huge seaplane hangar full of ships, a palace park with swans, and a beach a short bus ride away. Summer evenings stay light very late, and in December a Christmas market fills Town Hall Square.

Top things to do in Tallinn with kids

  1. Old town walls and towers Ages 4+

    Find Old Thomas, the soldier weather vane on the Town Hall, walk a stretch of the city wall between the towers and look over the rooftops from the Kohtuotsa and Patkuli viewing platforms.

  2. Seaplane Harbour Ages 4+

    Inside a giant concrete hangar kids can climb into the 1930s submarine Lembit, try ship and flight simulators, and explore the old icebreaker Suur Tõll moored outside.

  3. Kadriorg Park Ages 3+

    A short tram ride from the centre, this palace park has a swan pond, playgrounds and the Miia-Milla-Manda children's museum, where kids play shop, cook and dress up.

  4. Estonian Open Air Museum Ages 4+

    Wooden farmhouses, windmills and a village tavern stand in a seaside forest at Rocca al Mare, with crafts and horse-cart rides on summer days.

  5. Proto Invention Factory Ages 6+

    In the Noblessner harbour district, old inventions such as hot-air balloons and early cars come to life through virtual reality and hands-on machines.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Kiek in de Kök and Bastion Tunnels

    A cannon tower museum with guided walks through old defensive tunnels under Toompea hill.

  • Kalev Marzipan Room

    Small old town workshop where children paint their own marzipan figures to take home.

  • Tallinn TV Tower

    Ride the lift to a 170-metre viewing floor with glass floor panels and interactive exhibits about Estonia.

A simple first day

Morning
Enter the old town by Viru Gate and find Old Thomas on the Town Hall roof.
Afternoon
Walk through Kalamaja to the Seaplane Harbour and climb aboard the submarine.
Evening
Watch the sunset from the Patkuli viewing platform over the old town roofs.

Getting around

The old town is best on foot, while trams reach Kadriorg and the airport and buses go to Rocca al Mare and Pirita beach; buy a smart card (Ühiskaart) at a kiosk or a QR ticket online before boarding.

Food kids like

Try dark rye bread, pancakes with jam, kama (a sweet grain and yoghurt drink) and Kalev chocolate; lunch menus run around 12:00-15:00 and many cafes have children's corners.

Safety notes

  • Old town cobbles get slippery in rain and very icy in winter, so give children shoes with good grip and hold hands on the steep lanes.
  • Cruise ships bring large crowds to the old town around midday in summer, so agree a meeting point such as the Town Hall door.
  • Tap water is safe to drink, and traffic drives on the right, so teach children to look left first when crossing.
