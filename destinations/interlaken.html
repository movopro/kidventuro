🇨🇭 Interlaken with kids · Switzerland

Interlaken family guide

Interlaken lies on a flat strip of land between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, with the snowy Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau rising behind it. For families it works as a base camp: every morning a different train, boat or funicular takes you to waterfalls, cogwheel railways or a mountain lake, and every evening you are back in a calm, walkable town. Switzerland is expensive, but children's travel deals soften the cost.

Top things to do in Interlaken with kids

  1. Harder Kulm funicular Ages 3+

    A ten-minute funicular climbs from Interlaken Ost to a viewing platform hanging over both lakes, with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau straight ahead; it runs roughly April to November.

  2. Grindelwald First Ages 6+

    Take the gondola up, walk the First Cliff Walk along the rock face and continue on an easy trail of about an hour to the Bachalpsee lake; in summer older kids can ride mountain carts down.

  3. Lauterbrunnen waterfalls Ages 4+

    Watch Staubbach Falls drop off a cliff right in the village for free, then see Trümmelbach Falls thunder inside the mountain, reached by a tunnel lift and open from spring to autumn.

  4. Boat on Lake Brienz Ages 3+

    Board a lake boat at Interlaken Ost for Iseltwald or Giessbach, where an old funicular climbs to a huge waterfall behind a grand hotel; boats are included in most Swiss passes.

  5. Schynige Platte railway Ages 4+

    A cogwheel train running since 1893 climbs slowly from Wilderswil to an Alpine garden full of mountain flowers and easy short walks; the line operates only in summer and early autumn.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • St. Beatus Caves

    Lit limestone caves above Lake Thun with an underground path and the legend of a dragon.

  • Bödelibad indoor pool

    Interlaken's public pool with indoor swimming and a children's area for a wet afternoon.

  • Chocolate workshop

    Several shops in town run hands-on classes where kids make and decorate their own Swiss chocolate.

A simple first day

Morning
Walk across the Höhematte meadow and watch paragliders land with the Jungfrau behind them.
Afternoon
Ride the Harder Kulm funicular and spot both lakes from the platform.
Evening
Stroll along the Aare promenade and eat rösti in town.

Getting around

Trains from Interlaken Ost reach Lauterbrunnen, Grindelwald and Wilderswil in under 40 minutes, and the guest card from your hotel gives free local buses.

Food kids like

Rösti (crispy grated potato), cheese fondue, Älplermagronen (Alpine macaroni with apple sauce) and meringues with cream all please children; supermarkets such as Coop and Migros are good for picnic lunches.

Safety notes

  • The Aare river through Interlaken is cold and fast, so swim only at lake beaches and lidos, never in the river channel.
  • Mountain weather changes quickly and the sun is strong above 2,000 metres, so carry a jacket, sun cream and water on every trip up.
  • The Jungfraujoch at 3,454 metres can cause headaches and tiredness in young children, so go slowly and come down if anyone feels unwell.
