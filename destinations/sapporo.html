Sapporo family guide

Sapporo, on Japan's northern island of Hokkaido, turns into a snow city every winter. In early February the Snow Festival fills Odori Park with sculptures as big as buildings, and kids slide down snow ramps at the family site. Warm underground walkways link the centre, steaming bowls of miso ramen wait in every street, and a day trip brings the famous penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo.

Best ages

Every age loves the snow play and sculptures, while ages 6-12 cope best with long cold days outside; babies are fine with warm layers and short outings.

Stroller

Packed snow and ice make strollers hard work outside in winter, but the Chi-ka-ho underground walkway from Sapporo Station to Odori and Susukino is smooth with lifts. Many local families use a carrier or a small sled.

Top things to do in Sapporo with kids

  1. Sapporo Snow Festival Ages 3+

    For about a week in early February, Odori Park shows giant snow sculptures and Susukino glitters with ice carvings, while the Tsudome site has snow slides and rafting for kids.

  2. Mt. Moiwa Ropeway Ages 3+

    A ropeway and a small cable car climb to a summit with one of Japan's best night views; adults pay about JPY 2,100 return and children half.

  3. Takino Suzuran Hillside Park Ages 3+

    In winter this national park becomes a big snow playground with tubing hills, snowshoe trails and sledding, about an hour from the centre by subway and bus.

  4. Asahiyama Zoo penguin walk Ages 3+

    On winter days, king penguins waddle past visitors in a snowy line at this zoo in Asahikawa, about 85 minutes away by JR limited express train.

  5. Otaru day trip Ages 4+

    About 40 minutes away by JR, kids watch music boxes and glass being made beside the old canal, lit with candles during February's Snow Light Path.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Shiroi Koibito Park

    A cookie factory with a production line view and a decorate-your-own cookie class.

  • Sapporo Science Center

    Hands-on science galleries and a planetarium near Shin-Sapporo station, all indoors and warm.

  • Sapporo Olympic Museum

    At the Okurayama ski jump, try the jump simulator and ride the lift to the top.

A simple first day

Morning
Walk the Odori Park sculptures and peek at the Sapporo Clock Tower.
Afternoon
Warm up underground in Chi-ka-ho, then visit Shiroi Koibito Park.
Evening
Ride up Mt. Moiwa for the night view, then share miso ramen in Susukino.

Getting around

Three subway lines and a streetcar loop cover the city, and IC cards such as Kitaca or Suica work on all of them. JR trains reach Otaru in about 40 minutes and New Chitose Airport in about 40 minutes.

Food kids like

Kids usually love miso ramen from Ramen Yokocho, mild soup curry, grilled Genghis Khan lamb and rich Hokkaido milk soft-serve, even in winter. Lunch starts around 11:30 and dinner around 6 pm.

Safety notes

  • Pavements are often icy, so buy slip-on shoe spikes from a convenience store or drug store and teach kids to take small, flat penguin steps.
  • Days are often around -5 to -10°C with wind, but shops and trains are very warm, so dress in layers that come off easily.
  • Snow and icicles can slide off roofs without warning, so walk away from building edges after snowfall; tap water is safe to drink.
