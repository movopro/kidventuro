Why Barcelona works well as a kids’ activity adventure

Barcelona is full of bold shapes, bright colors and memorable street scenes. That makes it easy to turn sightseeing into a game instead of asking children to simply follow along. A mosaic color to spot, a balcony shape to count or a market ingredient to find gives kids a reason to stay engaged.

Kidventuro adapts activities to ages 4–12 and can reflect interests such as art, food, football, animals, mysteries or architecture. Quieter pages also help during flights, airport waits, restaurants and downtime.
