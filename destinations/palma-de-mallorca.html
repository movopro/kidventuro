Palma de Mallorca family guide

Palma is a proper city on a sunny bay, which makes it a good family base when you want beaches and something to do on a cloudy day. The Gothic cathedral sits right above the sea, a round castle watches from a pine hill, and a 1912 wooden train runs through orange groves to Sóller. Late spring and early autumn give warm sea water without the July crowds.

Top things to do in Palma de Mallorca with kids

  1. La Seu cathedral Ages 5+

    Inside, coloured light from the huge rose window falls on the floor and Gaudí's iron canopy hangs over the altar; adult entry is roughly €10, with reductions for children.

  2. Bellver Castle Ages 4+

    A rare round castle from the 1300s with a moat, a prison tower and views over the whole bay; entry is a few euros and free on Sundays.

  3. Sóller train and tram Ages all ages

    The 1912 wooden train crosses the mountains in about an hour, then an old open tram runs down to the port beach; adults pay roughly €20-30 each way, and the line often pauses for maintenance in December and January.

  4. Palma Aquarium Ages all ages

    One of Europe's deepest shark tanks plus a jungle zone and outdoor play area near Can Pastilla; booking online saves a few euros per ticket.

  5. City beaches Ages all ages

    Ciutat Jardí has shallow water and calm, sandy shallows for small children, while Can Pere Antoni is closest to the old town for a quick swim.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Coves del Drac

    Huge caves near Porto Cristo with an underground lake and a short boat ride with live music.

  • Mercat de l'Olivar

    Covered market with fish, fruit and tapas counters where kids can graze and look around.

  • Es Baluard museum

    Modern art inside the old city walls, with a free terrace and regular family workshops.

A simple first day

Morning
Visit the cathedral early and walk through Parc de la Mar beside its lake.
Afternoon
Head to Ciutat Jardí for a swim and lunch at a beach chiringuito.
Evening
Wander the old town lanes for tapas and an ensaïmada for dessert.

Getting around

EMT city buses run along the bay and to the airport, and old town sights are walkable; hire a car or use TIB buses for Sóller, the caves and quieter coves.

Food kids like

Kids tend to love ensaïmada spiral pastries, pa amb oli (bread with tomato and oil), croquetas and ice cream; locals lunch around 13:30-15:30 and dine from 20:30.

Safety notes

  • Summer sun is strong from June to September, so use high-factor sunscreen, hats and shade between noon and 4 pm.
  • Respect beach flags and choose bays with lifeguards, as wind can bring currents even on calm-looking days.
  • Tap water in Palma is safe but tastes of minerals, so many families buy large bottles; watch bags on crowded buses and beaches.
