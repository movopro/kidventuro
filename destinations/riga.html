🇱🇻 Riga with kids · Latvia

Riga family guide

Riga is the biggest city in the Baltics, but its old town is small enough to cross in fifteen minutes and full of things children notice: a black cat on a rooftop, four animal musicians standing on each other's backs, golden roosters on church spires. Add a giant food market in old airship hangars and a sandy beach half an hour away by train, and it is an easy, good-value city break.

Top things to do in Riga with kids

  1. Old town animal hunt Ages 4+

    Find the Town Musicians of Bremen statue, the black cats on the Cat House roof and the House of the Blackheads, then take the lift up St Peter's Church tower for a view over the river.

  2. Riga Central Market Ages 4+

    Five huge pavilions built from old zeppelin hangars sell smoked fish, berries, honey and pastries, so let kids choose a picnic and pay a few euros.

  3. Jūrmala beach day Ages 3+

    Trains from Riga Central Station reach Majori in about 30-40 minutes, where a long sandy beach with shallow water and pine forest awaits in summer.

  4. Ethnographic Open-Air Museum Ages 4+

    On the shore of Lake Jugla, old farmsteads, windmills and a wooden church spread through pine woods, and craft days in summer let kids try old skills.

  5. Riga Zoo in Mežaparks Ages 3+

    A forest-park zoo with a tropical house and animals from Baltic woods to warmer lands, reached by tram from the centre and next to big playgrounds.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Riga Motor Museum

    Hundreds of cars, motorbikes and racing machines, including vehicles once driven by Soviet leaders.

  • Laima Chocolate Museum

    Learn how Latvia's best-known chocolate is made and decorate your own bar in a workshop.

  • Latvian Museum of Natural History

    Central museum with stuffed animals, skeletons and minerals that suits a couple of indoor hours.

A simple first day

Morning
Walk the old town from the Freedom Monument to the Cat House and the Town Musicians statue.
Afternoon
Graze for lunch at the Central Market, then ride the lift up St Peter's tower.
Evening
Feed the ducks along the city canal in Bastejkalns Park and have dinner at a Lido.

Getting around

The old town is walkable and Rīgas Satiksme trams, trolleybuses and buses cover the rest of the city, while suburban trains run to Jūrmala from Riga Central Station.

Food kids like

Look for pīrāgi (little bacon buns), potato pancakes, grey peas with bacon and rye bread pudding with cream; lunch is around 12:00-14:00 and cafes open early.

Safety notes

  • Baltic Sea beaches at Jūrmala are shallow but can have currents on windy days, so swim near lifeguard posts and keep little ones within reach.
  • Cobbles and tram rails in the old town catch small wheels and scooters, so walk with children on the narrow streets.
  • Tap water is treated and safe, though many locals prefer bottled for taste, and winter pavements can be icy from December to March.
