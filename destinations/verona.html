🇮🇹 Verona with kids · Italy

Verona family guide

Verona is a walkable, river-bend city with a Roman arena still used for summer opera, a balcony linked to Romeo and Juliet and some of Italy's best gelato. It also makes an easy base for Lake Garda: Gardaland, Italy's biggest theme park, is about 30 minutes away. Families often mix two days in town with a lake day of boats, beaches and castle walls in Sirmione.

Top things to do in Verona with kids

  1. Arena di Verona Ages all ages

    Walk the inside of a 2,000-year-old amphitheatre and climb the top rows; daytime visits are shorter in summer opera season, so check hours before going.

  2. Gardaland Ages 3+

    Italy's largest theme park near Castelnuovo del Garda, open roughly April to early November plus Christmas weekends; online tickets are about €40-55 and kids under 1 m go free.

  3. Castel San Pietro funicular Ages all ages

    A short funicular climbs from the river to a terrace with the best view of the city's red roofs and bridges, especially near sunset.

  4. Torre dei Lamberti Ages 5+

    Take the lift most of the way up the 84 m medieval tower above Piazza delle Erbe, then climb the last steps to see the Alps on clear days.

  5. Sirmione on Lake Garda Ages all ages

    A castle surrounded by water, shallow pebble beaches and gelato; reach it by train to Desenzano or Peschiera then bus, or by lake ferry in summer.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Museo di Storia Naturale

    Fossils from nearby Bolca, minerals and stuffed animals in a palace near the river.

  • Castelvecchio Museum

    A brick castle with armour, swords and a bridge with swallowtail battlements to walk across.

  • Sea Life Gardaland

    Aquarium next to Gardaland with a walk-through tunnel, good for a wet lake day.

A simple first day

Morning
Explore the arena early, then walk Via Mazzini to Juliet's courtyard before crowds build.
Afternoon
Ride up Torre dei Lamberti and share gelato in Piazza delle Erbe.
Evening
Take the funicular to Castel San Pietro and watch the lights come on over the Adige.

Getting around

Walk the old town from Piazza Bra; for Gardaland take the 15-minute train to Peschiera del Garda and the park's free shuttle bus.

Food kids like

Kids like pizza, tortellini from nearby Valeggio, gnocchi and pandoro, the star-shaped Christmas cake invented in Verona; lunch is 12:30-14:30 and dinner rarely before 19:30.

Safety notes

  • Summer days around Lake Garda and in the arena reach 32-35 °C, so carry water and hats and rest in the shade after lunch.
  • Some streets in the old town still allow scooters and delivery vans, so hold small hands near Piazza Bra and Via Mazzini.
  • On Lake Garda, swim only at guarded beaches and keep kids close as the lakebed can drop quickly; tap water is safe.
