🇨🇷 La Fortuna with kids · Costa Rica

La Fortuna family guide

La Fortuna is a small town at the foot of Arenal, a near-perfect volcano cone that often wears a cap of cloud. Families walk over old lava flows, cross hanging bridges through the treetops, swim below a tall waterfall and end the day in warm volcanic hot springs. At night, guides with torches find red-eyed tree frogs, and sloths are a common sight by day.

Top things to do in La Fortuna with kids

  1. La Fortuna Waterfall Ages 6+

    Walk down roughly 500 steps to a 70-metre waterfall and swim in the calmer side pools; adults pay about USD 18-20 and young children enter free or cheaper.

  2. Arenal Volcano National Park Ages 4+

    Easy trails cross the 1968 lava flow, where kids clamber on black rocks and look up at the cone; go early, before clouds hide the summit.

  3. Mistico Hanging Bridges Ages 4+

    A 3 km loop of suspension bridges over the rainforest canopy, best with a guide whose telescope spots sloths, toucans and sleeping bats.

  4. Hot springs afternoon Ages 3+

    Resorts such as Baldi, Paradise and Tabacón have warm river or pool soaks, some with kids' pools and slides; day passes vary widely in price.

  5. Night frog walk Ages 5+

    Guided night walks at reserves like Arenal Natura reveal red-eyed tree frogs, glass frogs and sleeping birds by torchlight in about two hours.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Rainforest chocolate tour

    A covered farm tour from cacao pod to tasting bar, hands-on for kids.

  • Hot springs in the rain

    Warm pools feel even better in a light shower, as long as there is no thunder.

  • Arenal EcoZoo

    A serpentarium near El Castillo where snakes, frogs and reptiles are seen safely behind glass.

A simple first day

Morning
Walk the lava trails in Arenal Volcano National Park while the cone is clear.
Afternoon
Swim below La Fortuna Waterfall, then relax in town.
Evening
Join a night frog walk, then soak in a hot spring.

Getting around

A rental car gives the most freedom, and the main roads are paved. Shared shuttles connect San José in about 3-3.5 hours, and most tours include pick-up from La Fortuna hotels.

Food kids like

Breakfast is gallo pinto with eggs and plantain, lunch a casado plate at a small soda, and fresh fruit batidos are everywhere. Dinners are early, around 6-7 pm, because darkness falls soon after six.

Safety notes

  • Rivers and waterfall pools rise quickly after heavy rain, so swim only where guides or signs say it is safe and stay out after storms.
  • Snakes, bullet ants and spiny plants live along the trails, so keep kids on the path, wear closed shoes and never pick up animals.
  • Roads are narrow and wet, and it gets dark around 6 pm all year, so drive in daylight; tap water in town is generally safe.
