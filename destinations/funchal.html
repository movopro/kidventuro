Funchal family guide

Funchal climbs steeply up from the Atlantic on Madeira, an island with spring-like weather most of the year. Families ride a cable car into the hills, slide back down in a wicker basket steered by men in straw hats, and walk flat water channels called levadas through ancient laurel forest. Whales and dolphins swim offshore, and the New Year fireworks over the bay are famous.

Top things to do in Funchal with kids

  1. Funchal cable car to Monte Ages all ages

    A 15-minute ride from the Zona Velha seafront up to Monte, over red roofs and banana gardens; adults pay roughly €14-17 one way, children about half.

  2. Monte toboggan ride Ages 4+

    Two carreiros in straw hats push and steer a wicker basket two kilometres downhill on the road; since May 2026 it costs about €30 for one rider or €40 for two.

  3. Monte Palace Tropical Garden Ages all ages

    Koi ponds, tile panels, giant ferns and mineral displays on a steep hillside garden at the top of the cable car; children under 15 enter free.

  4. Levada dos Balcões Ages 4+

    One of Madeira's easiest walks, about 1.5 km each way from Ribeiro Frio along a water channel to a viewpoint where finches eat from your hand.

  5. Whale and dolphin boat trip Ages 4+

    Boats leave Funchal marina daily and often meet common dolphins and pilot whales; a two- to three-hour trip costs roughly €35-60 per person.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • Museu da Baleia

    Whale museum in Caniçal with life-size models and interactive whaling history.

  • Aquário da Madeira

    Small aquarium in an old fort at Porto Moniz, next to the natural lava pools.

  • Mercado dos Lavradores

    Covered market with strange tropical fruits, fish stalls and flower sellers to explore.

A simple first day

Morning
Explore the Mercado dos Lavradores and taste a passion fruit or banana.
Afternoon
Ride the cable car to Monte, visit the gardens and slide down by toboggan.
Evening
Walk the Zona Velha painted doors street and share bolo do caco for dinner.

Getting around

Funchal's yellow city buses climb to Monte and the hills; for levadas and Porto Moniz hire a small car or join a tour, as mountain roads are steep and winding.

Food kids like

Kids usually enjoy bolo do caco flatbread with garlic butter, espetada beef skewers, fried maize cubes (milho frito) and tiny Madeira bananas; dinner is from about 19:00.

Safety notes

  • Levada paths can have long drops on one side, so keep children on the inside, hold hands at narrow parts and skip routes rated difficult.
  • Atlantic swells hit the rocky shore hard, so swim in the Lido pools, Porto Moniz natural pools or guarded beaches only.
  • Weather changes fast in the mountains, so carry a rain jacket and warm layer even when Funchal is sunny; tap water is safe.
