Okinawa family guide

Okinawa feels like a different Japan: warm sea, coral reefs, a slower island pace and food that mixes Japanese, Chinese and American influences. Families usually base in Naha for the castle, markets and monorail, then drive north for a day at the Churaumi Aquarium, where whale sharks glide past a glass wall taller than a house. Calm, shallow beaches make it easy with younger children.

Top things to do in Okinawa with kids

  1. Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium Ages 2+

    Watch whale sharks and manta rays in the huge Kuroshio Sea tank, then catch the free dolphin show outside in Ocean Expo Park. Tickets are ¥2,180 for adults, ¥710 for school-age children and free under 6; it is about two hours' drive north of Naha.

  2. Kerama Islands day trip Ages 5+

    High-speed ferries from Tomari Port reach Tokashiki or Zamami in roughly 35-50 minutes, landing near beaches with clear water and sea turtles. Book ahead and check the forecast, as sailings are cancelled in rough seas.

  3. Shurijo Castle Park Ages 5+

    The red palace of the old Ryukyu kings burned in 2019 and is being rebuilt, and visitors can watch the craftsmen at work. Gates, stone walls and viewpoints are free; the paid area costs ¥400 for adults and less for children.

  4. Kouri Island Ages 3+

    Drive across the 2 km Kouri Bridge over turquoise water to a small island with shallow beaches and the heart-shaped Heart Rock. It pairs easily with the aquarium day, which is about 30 minutes away.

  5. Makishi Public Market and Kokusai-dori Ages 4+

    Pick colourful fish on the ground floor of the market and have them cooked upstairs, then walk Kokusai-dori for shisa figures and Blue Seal ice cream. Expect about ¥1,500-3,000 per person for a market meal.

Rainy-day and cold-day options

  • DMM Kariyushi Aquarium

    A modern, compact aquarium near the airport with touch pools, sloths and colourful reef fish under one roof.

  • Okinawa World and Gyokusendo Cave

    Walk a lit underground path through stalactites, then watch an Eisa drum show in the covered village.

  • Okinawa Prefectural Museum

    Ryukyu history, sea fossils and a hands-on discovery room in Naha's Omoromachi district.

A simple first day

Morning
Ride the monorail to Shuri and walk the castle walls while it is still cool.
Afternoon
Head back to Kokusai-dori for lunch at Makishi Public Market and a Blue Seal ice cream.
Evening
Watch the sunset at Naminoue Beach in Naha, a short taxi ride away, before an early dinner.

Getting around

The Yui Rail monorail links Naha Airport with Kokusai-dori and Shuri, and is simple with children. For the aquarium and northern beaches a rental car is easiest, taking about two hours on the expressway; otherwise use the Yanbaru Express bus.

Food kids like

Okinawa soba has thick wheat noodles in a mild pork broth that most children like, and taco rice and sata andagi doughnuts are easy wins. Many restaurants serve dinner from about 17:00, and families often eat early to beat the queues.

Safety notes

  • The sun is strong even in spring, so use rash guards and hats and plan beach time for mornings and late afternoons.
  • Box jellyfish appear from about June to September, so swim only at beaches with jellyfish nets and lifeguards and follow posted flags.
  • Typhoons can hit from July to October and stop ferries and flights, so keep flexible days; if you drive, cars keep left and under-6s must use a child seat.
